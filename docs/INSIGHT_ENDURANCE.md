# Endurance reconstruction plan

## Phase 1: Conditional scenario (implemented)

Read existing per-fight rotation and replay records without modifying parsing or metrics. Attach up to 12 endurance evidence records to a selected player's recorded down moments. Anchor on an exact `Dodge` cast before the down, identify vigor/weakness changes, and display a conditional endurance range in Insight's evidence workbench. Missing anchors, unsupported specializations, survival interruptions and tied dodge/down timestamps suppress the calculation.

Keep actual availability unknown. A range is not a probability. Version the assumed rules and include their review date and source. The current reference scenario is not a historical balance-patch certification. Overlapping vigor/weakness is deliberately unresolved pending formula verification.

## Phase 2: Verified rules and event coverage

Preserve game build and mode per fight. Audit EI dodge IDs and profession-specific replacements, recorded start/end awareness, missing rotations, vigor/weakness coverage and Resistance interactions. Add tested historical cost/capacity/regeneration rules per build. Do not infer equipment from absent procs or assume ally equipment is known.

Model timestamped endurance gains from food, sigils, traits, relics and ally skills only when recipient and amount are established. Unknown refunds remain explicit. Separate regeneration caps from instantaneous gains. Check the total reconstructed dodges against per-fight defensive counts, without using aggregate counts to invent timestamps.

## Phase 3: Validation and probabilities

Collect synchronized POV endurance-bar footage and logs with consent. Evaluate held-out fights, classes and patches. Measure interval coverage, false availability claims and probability calibration before publishing a percentage. A Monte Carlo scenario frequency is not a calibrated chance.

## Phase 4: Avoidability

Combine endurance with action restrictions, animation state, attack timing and verified evadeability. Distinguish having a dodge resource from the opportunity to use it and from preventing death. Retain counterevidence, unresolved alternatives, and replay navigation.

Reference: https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Endurance (reviewed 2026-09-09).
