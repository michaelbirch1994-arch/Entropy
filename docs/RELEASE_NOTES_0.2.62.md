# Entropy v0.2.62

## Intelligence command surface

- Critical Events are now promoted to the primary Intelligence workspace directly beneath the Intelligence headline.
- The selected event's evidence/replay state is displayed beside the Critical Event Feed on wide displays and stacked beneath it on narrower layouts.
- Selecting a critical event immediately updates the evidence inspector, keeping the event and its supporting state visually connected.

## Streamlined fight scoping

- The large Fight Breakdown card wall has been replaced by a compact horizontal fight-scope rail.
- Fight selection remains available without pushing Entropy's strongest Intelligence features below the fold.
- Critical-event kind filtering remains available in the primary command surface.

## Clearer Intelligence hierarchy

- Primary evidence now comes first: Critical Events and Replay/Event Evidence.
- Interpretation and supporting analytics follow below: pressure/readiness, findings/actions, priority engagements, and deeper evidence review.
- The duplicate lower Critical Event Feed has been removed; deep evidence now focuses on the broader timeline and findings chain.

## Core values preserved

- No detector logic, aggregation rules, Replay calculations, evidence semantics, or source-data meanings were changed in this release.
- Replay, Death Recap, detailed metrics, tables, and other core Entropy views remain the authoritative inspection surfaces.
- Intelligence continues to connect and interpret existing evidence rather than replacing the underlying viewer.
